ConsultCTA: placed at the bottom of technical posts and on the about page. Outline button, not a filled banner. Present, not pushy.

```jsx
<ConsultCTA
  body="Example copy: replace with the real availability on the day you publish."
  buttonLabel="Discuss a project"
/>
```

The body is availability language, and it must be true on the day you publish. Wording such as "I take on a small number of engagements each quarter" is an example only, not a current claim. The link is at least 44px tall, and its label carries no arrow glyph.
