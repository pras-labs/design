ArticleMeta: byline row shown under every post title and in list previews.

```jsx
<ArticleMeta date="Jul 18, 2026" readTime="11 min read" tags={['kubernetes', 'gitops']} />
```

Always exact calendar dates: the voice guideline explicitly rejects "2 days ago" framing. Hashtags are static labels, not links (use `Tag` where a topic is clickable). The "/" separators are decorative (`aria-hidden`). The sample values above are sample text, not a published article.
